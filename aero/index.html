The air, checked.

The VX-1's downforce and drag were targets, written into its specification and never computed. The Nyx's lift and stability came from our own vortex-lattice code and a textbook drag polar. Here both go through OpenFOAM, the open-source CFD suite used across industry and research, on the geometry exactly as it is built, and every number we published is set beside what the solver says.
